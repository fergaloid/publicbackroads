This is a list of product and service backlogs and routemaps/roadmaps opened in the public domain by public sector organisations around the world. And some charities and community-interest organisations at the end.

Here's a post explaining [why opening backlogs and roadmaps is a good thing](https://public.digital/pd-insights/blog/2024/07/filling-in-the-gaps-the-case-for-public-backlogs-roadmaps) that we all benefit from and calling for more.

And this is a post about [preferring the term 'routemaps' to 'roadmaps'](https://rossferg.medium.com/making-the-case-for-product-and-service-routemaps-ccc1d78c7111).

I'm keeping the list updated. If you know of an open public sector backlog or roadmap that should be included, [suggest it through this form]({{ site.github.repository_url }}/issues/new?template=suggest-a-link.yml).
